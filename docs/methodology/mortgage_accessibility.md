# Mortgage Accessibility Score Methodology

## Purpose
To determine the ease with which citizens can access housing finance and mortgage facilities.

## Data Sources
- **Derived Indicator**: Housing Accessibility Index
- **Source D**: Bank Indonesia (BI Rate / National Interest Rate)

## Formula
`Mortgage Accessibility = (0.60 × AccessibilityIndex) + (0.40 × InterestRateScoreInverse)`
*The national BI rate is normalized inversely, meaning lower rates yield a higher accessibility score.*

## Interpretation
A high score implies a favorable environment for borrowing, driven by strong regional purchasing power (Accessibility) combined with a dovish, low-interest macroeconomic climate.

## Limitations
The score assumes uniform lending standards across all provinces and does not account for localized unbanked populations or variations in provincial banking infrastructure.

## Academic Justification
Mortgage uptake is fundamentally a function of two constraints: the borrower's capacity to pay (captured by the Accessibility Index at 60%) and the cost of capital (captured by the BI Rate at 40%). This dual-factor model aligns with IMF frameworks for assessing financial inclusion in real estate.
